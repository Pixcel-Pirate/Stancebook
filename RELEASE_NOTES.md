## What's new

- **New look:** Stancebook is now a dark, modern spellbook. Each class gets its own banner in its class color, and the whole book re-dyes when you switch class.
- Class picker moved to a rail down the left side, with the sections as tabs under the class banner.
- Cleaner spell list, macro page, talent trees and guides. Works better on phones too (no zoom when you tap the search box, and it fits around the notch).
- All macros, abilities, talents, and your saved favorites, action bars and builds are unchanged.
