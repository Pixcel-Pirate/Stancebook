## What's new

- **Change counts work like unread:** a class's change bubble goes away once you click that class or open its patch notes, and stays gone until a newer patch changes that class again.
- **See your class's changes at a glance:** the top bar says how many changes your class got in the latest patch (or "no changes"), and each class icon shows a small count bubble when the latest patch changed it.
- **Every patch checked and dated:** the Patch notes tab now shows the full date of every update and lists two updates it was missing (Oct 18 and Oct 23). Oct 30 and Oct 31 are now separate, each change sits under the update that made it, and updates with no official notes are marked "From game data".
- **Simpler patch bar:** the bar at the top now just names the latest patch update, with a button straight to your class's patch notes. The Patch notes tab opens with a short description instead of counts and chips.
- **Patch notes for every class:** a new Patch notes tab lists every WoW Forever change to your class in plain words, grouped by patch (Sept 24, Oct 1, Oct 8, Oct 15, Oct 26) plus what Forever changed from original Classic. The All notes link in the patch strip opens it.
- **WoW Forever BIS lists:** the Armory's best-in-slot lists are now WoW Forever's level 60 lists for all 28 specs (from The Forever Era, built from the Forever beta client), replacing the Classic addon lists. Items are tagged New in Forever, Changed, or Not in beta client, and links open the item's Forever page. They're forecasts until Forever reaches level 60.
- **Best in slot lists in the Armory:** pre-raid and raid BIS for every class and spec (25 specs, from the Best In Slot Classic addon's lists), with the weapon picks for your race. Every item shows where it drops or how it's made. Hover for the tooltip, or click the name to open it on Wowhead. The dressing room is gone.
- **Gear sets in 3D:** every Classic set in the Armory has a View set in 3D on Wowhead button. It opens the set on Wowhead, where you can see it worn by any race and gender.
- **Races in 3D:** the Races tab has a View races in 3D on Wowhead button. It opens Wowhead's Classic dressing room, where you pick the race and gender and drag to rotate. Skyborne is Forever-only, so it has no model there.
- **New Armory tab:** every Classic gear set (Dungeon Sets 1 and 2, Tier 1 to 3, Zul'Gurub, Ahn'Qiraj, PvP, Arathi Basin, crafted and dungeon sets) with the real icon for every piece, every epic weapon and shield, and WoW Forever's new Hyjal sets with their set bonuses. Filter by cloth, leather, mail or plate, weapons, or shields, pick a race to see only what its classes can use, or show only your class's gear. Hover a piece for where it drops.
- **Every ability, every class:** the Abilities tab now shows each class's full WoW Forever spellbook, not just the spells the macros use. New Forever spells are in (Aspect of the Viper, Frost Blast, Revoke, Explorer Imp, Call of the Ancestors and more), and ones Forever removed are gone (Lightwell, single-target Blessing of Kings, Tiger's Fury, Tranquil Air Totem and others).
- **Talent abilities marked:** every ability you get from a talent says "Talent" with its tree and the points it needs. Filter by Trainer, Talent or Pet & items.
- **New Races tab:** all races, including both Skyborne factions, with their racials as of the Oct 26 patch. Races that can be priests show their two priest-only spells.
- **Macro fixes:** the paladin blessing macro uses Greater Blessing of Kings, the mage decurse macro uses Remove Lesser Curse, and the Lightwell macro is removed.
