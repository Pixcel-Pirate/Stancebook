## What's new

- **Nov 2 launch patch:** Blizzard's launch notes for every class are in the Patch notes tab and the Forever notes, including Rogue Shank and Poised, harder-hitting Feral and Hunter shots, and shared trap cooldowns.
- **Racials:** the racial note now covers the Nov 2 fixes (Tauren Warrior Strength, Undead Dark Sacrifice, Skyborne stats).
- **Removed spells cleaned up:** the Berserk, Binding Heal and Lava Burst macros are gone, and Salvation, Blessing of Light, Frost Blast and Lava Burst left the Abilities list. The Ambush macro now uses Ghostly Strike (Cutthroat was removed).
- **Talents re-checked** against wowforevertalents: Shaman gets Call of Thunder and Lightning Mastery, Mage Precision, and a Druid Restoration row swap. Rogue talents update once the talent site has the Nov 2 trees.
