## What's new

- **Nov 10 tuning:** Blizzard's first live tuning pass is in the Patch notes tab: tanking Hunter pets and Voidwalker, Affliction DoTs next to Wrack, and Enhancement back on Strength.
- **Nov 6 hotfixes:** Voidwalker health, Soul Siphon and Wrack, Druid Bloom, and Mage Arcane Blast fixes.
- **Nov 3-4 hotfixes:** Fury's Raging Blows is replaced by Reckless Swings and Improved Whirlwind, tank stances make more threat, and Wrack hits much harder.
- **Shaman:** Totemic Projection is now Totemic Call (pulls totems to you), so the macro no longer uses the cursor. Riptide is a HoT on a 12-second cooldown.
- **Talents re-checked:** Warrior Fury and all three Rogue trees now match the launch talents (Venom, Boot Knife, Quick Getaway, Coup de Grace and more).
